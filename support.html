Frequently asked questions

How do I place an order?

Go to Home, pick your social media platform, choose the service (followers, likes, views…) and the quality type, paste your link, enter the quantity and press PURCHASE. Your wallet is charged the shown price and the order starts right away.

How do I add funds?

Press the + in the top bar or ADD FUNDS on your Wallet. Choose MTN MoMo, Orange Money or crypto, enter the amount and your number/address, then approve the payment. Your balance is credited as soon as the payment provider confirms it.

My order shows “Pending”. Is that normal?

Yes. Pending means the order is being processed. Delivery time depends on the service (it is shown before purchase). You can follow the progress on the Orders page — it refreshes automatically while orders are pending.

What happens if my order is canceled?

If the provider cancels an order, the full amount is automatically refunded to your wallet. You will see a green “Refund — Canceled and Refunded” entry in your Wallet activity.

Why do I need a public account/post?

Services can only be delivered to public links. Private profiles or non-public posts cannot receive followers or engagement, so the order would fail.

What is the difference between Average and High quality?

Average quality is cheaper, starts faster and has a shorter guarantee with more drop. High quality costs more, uses better-looking/real accounts and keeps followers longer. For followers: bots are cheap but don't last; real accounts stay forever and are expensive.

How do I use the Referral Program?

Open the Referral Program page and share your code or link. When a referred friend adds funds, you earn a commission on the deposit — it is added to your affiliate balance in the Wallet.

Can I use the API?

Yes. Open the API page to generate your personal API key. You can then place and check orders programmatically — see the examples on that page.
